KTX & AREX / RAIL CHECKS

Know which train stops where.

KORAIL says KTX tickets open at 07:00 two months before travel (VisitKorea says usually one month). On Day 16, board the AREX All-Stop Train at Seoul Station for Hongik Univ. Station—the AREX Express runs non-stop to Incheon Airport.
