Outbound route

Start on foot from L7 Haeundae toward Haeundae Beach and the west-end Dongbaekseom peninsula (or use Line 2 Haeundae → Dongbaek Exit 1). After Nurimaru APEC House, head to the east end of Haeundae Beach for Blueline Park Mipo Station; the brief mentions taking a bus to Mipo, though Mipo is also walkable along the beachfront and no bus number or duration is verified.

FIELD NOTE / DAY 10

The brief instructs travelers to 'book round trip tickets' on the Sky Capsule, but Haeundae Blueline Park sells Sky Capsule rides as one-way departures only (Mipo→Cheongsapo or Cheongsapo→Mipo). BUSAN X the SKY is listed both here as optional and on Day 12 at 10:00–11:30.
