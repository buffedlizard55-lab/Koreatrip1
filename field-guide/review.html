HOW TO READ THIS PAGE

Evidence is not the same as context.

These items are published for post-publication review, not held back for approval. The source outline ends with Day 9 arrival in Busan; the earlier experimental Friday–Sunday Busan/Seoul-return draft has been withdrawn. An official 'context only' link points to an operator or city directory but does NOT verify the specific unverified claim. Missing addresses, hours, prices and bookings are intentionally left blank.

Route corrected means an official source supports the correction. Context only · not proof means the linked official site does not establish the particular claim.
