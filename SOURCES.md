# Official-source evidence ledger

Checked 2026-10-21 UTC. This is a research snapshot, not a guarantee for a future travel date.

## Method and limitations

- Confirmed columns contain only facts explicitly observed in official operator, merchant, government or tourism-board pages / official-domain search excerpts.
- Every row was reviewed for identity, hours, location, prices and transport claims. A review does not mean all facts were confirmed. Unestablished values remain blank.
- No entry is marked fully verified. Partial means some fields have supporting evidence; the findings state the remaining gaps.
- Original traveler claims remain under the original-note disclosure. They are NOT verified columns. No USD conversion, overall budget, booking, transit duration or travel year has been invented.
- Generic Seoul and Busan directories are context-only links for unidentified entries. They do not satisfy venue-specific verification and are labeled as such throughout the site. It is impossible to supply a verified official business URL for a business whose identity has not been established.
- The official L7 location URL initially tried returned an error; it is not linked. The current hotel home page provided the address and standard check-in/out in its Location section (retrieved chunk 1).
- Stanford's former domain redirected to the current hotel group. Its landing page confirmed identity, but not checkout. Address came from official Seoul tourism.
- Eternalog's direct page returned sparse dynamic content; address evidence is from its official-domain search excerpt.
- Date mismatch is a local Gregorian calendar calculation: 2006-11-02 was Thursday; 2026-11-02 is Monday; 2026-10-31 is Saturday. This is not confirmation of a year.
- Search exploration included Stanford, Bukchon restrictions, COEX, Kyoja, DDP, Onion, Jayeondo, Eternalog, Solsot, Hanjungsun, Blue Elephant, MARGESHERWOOD, ADERERROR, Rain Report and KORAIL. Third-party results were not used to populate fact fields. Other unresolved named entities remain unverified; no claim of exhaustive official-web coverage is made.
