Sat Oct 31 – Mon Nov 9, 2026**, published as a GitHub Pages site:

## Verification policy

Every hour, price and closure on the site is either (a) confirmed on an official source
(operator/venue/government/Visit Korea/Visit Seoul/Visit Busan/MICHELIN Guide) with a link,
or (b) left blank and flagged **Unverified**. Nothing is inferred or guessed.
Prices are shown in official KRW; USD figures from the original document are flagged as approximations.

## Key finding

The original document scheduled **Gyeongbokgung Palace on a Tuesday — its weekly closing day**.
Days 2 and 3 are swapped on the site (palace → Monday Nov 2; N Seoul Tower → Tuesday Nov 3).
See `flagged.html` §1 for evidence and how to request a different fix.
