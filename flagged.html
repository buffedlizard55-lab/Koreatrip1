Critical Planning Error — Day 3 (Tuesday)

Gyeongbokgung Palace scheduled on Tuesday — Palace is closed every Tuesday

Source itinerary: Day 3 = Tuesday, Nov 3 (2006). Activity = Gyeongbokgung Palace.

Verified rule: Gyeongbokgung Palace is closed every Tuesday all year. If Tuesday is a public holiday, it opens that Tuesday and closes the next non-holiday instead — it does NOT stay open automatically.

Verification sources: Royal Palaces official — Hours & Admissions; VisitSeoul — Gyeongbokgung; VisitKorea.

Action required: Move Day 3 palace visit to Wednesday (Nov 4), Thursday (Nov 5), Friday (Nov 6), Saturday (Nov 7), Sunday (Nov 8), or Monday (Nov 9) — or verify if Nov 3, 2006 was a special holiday exception (unlikely; check Korean calendar). This blocks the Day 3 plan.

Secondary note: Changing of the Guard (10:00 / 14:00) is also not held on Tuesdays.
