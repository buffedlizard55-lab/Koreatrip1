## Method

- `src/data/sources.json` is the source registry. Each place in `src/data/places.json` carries the official sources for its displayed fields; each transit direction in `src/data/itinerary.json` carries sources; each finding in `src/data/review.json` carries evidence or a **context-only** research link. An official city homepage alone does not verify a named merchant.
- Operator pages are preferred for opening policies, fares and ticket sales; Seoul/Busan/Korea official tourism bodies are used for venue listings and access. When two official pages disagree, suppress the disputed value on the site rather than silently choose one.
- `null` means no supported value; the website renders an empty marker labeled **Unverified**. Planning periods (“morning”, “afternoon”) are editorial suggestions, **not** merchant hours. A published schedule is not confirmation of a special-day opening or a purchase.
- Source links were individually read for the claims recorded below. The live external sites may change after this snapshot; refresh before traveling. Internal link and data validation runs via `python3 scripts/check.py`.
