## Method

- `src/data/sources.json` is the source registry. Each place in `src/data/places.json` carries official sources for its displayed fields; each hotel-based transit leg in `src/data/itinerary.json` carries sources; each item in `src/data/review.json` carries evidence or a **context-only** research link. A city directory does **not** prove a named merchant exists. Source data from another session is useful for the *submitted outline*, not a substitute for independently checked official claims.
- Operators are preferred for opening policies, fares and ticket sales; Seoul/Busan/Korea tourism authorities are used for listings and access. When two official pages disagree, suppress the disputed value rather than arbitrarily picking one.
- `null` means no supported value; the site renders **— Unverified**. Suggested periods (“morning”, “afternoon”) are editorial, **not** merchant hours. A published schedule is not a purchased ticket or guaranteed future-day opening. Busan places marked **After Day 9 · unassigned extension** are research options, never Day 7/8 Busan stops.
- Direct official pages were read for the claims below. The live pages can change; recheck before travel. `python3 scripts/check.py --build --out field-guide` validates the local ledger, row-linked sources and all internal URLs/anchors; it does not verify future openings.
