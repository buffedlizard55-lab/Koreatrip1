🧭 Planning rules used on this page

  • Only operator/government-published times and fares are printed. Subway door-to-door times are intentionally blank — they depend on walking speed, transfers, and time of day, and no official source publishes them for these specific trips.
  • Use Naver Map (or Kakao Map/KakaoMetro) for live routing in Korea; Google Maps transit/walking directions are limited in Korea. (General traveler guidance — verify app availability before departure.)
  • A rechargeable transit card is the normal way to pay for subway/bus in Seoul and Busan — confirm current card products and fares on arrival at the airport/station (not verified here to avoid stale figures).
